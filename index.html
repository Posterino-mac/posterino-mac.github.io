Posterino

Create posters, collages, calendars, and postcards with effortless precision. Version 6.3.6 delivers enhanced customization through stylish templates with grids of identical or varied image boxes. Resize and reshape boxes freely, auto-fill with your photos in one click, or drag and drop manually.

Instant Workflow

Adding, removing, or creating image objects triggers immediate re-layout. Improved throughout for seamless editing, now with Retina support and a sandboxed architecture.

Rich Content Library

Discover new templates, frames, backgrounds, and text captions for every image. Advanced layouts include circular options and grid lines for precise manual placement.

Smart Automation

Automatic styling aligns and enhances your images effortlessly. A persistent crop editor with fine-tuning and custom auto-crop modes streamlines every adjustment.

Seamless Sharing

Upload directly to Facebook, Twitter, Flickr, or send via Message and AirDrop. Export to iPhoto, email, or craft custom e-cards with stamps and postmarks. Pixel-perfect exports at 72 DPI with scalable options up to 4×.
